🧩 What Exactly Is session-hub?

Think of session-hub as a shared window into your team's AI-powered coding work. It watches the sessions your teammates have with Claude Code and Cursor — those are AI tools that help write code. With session-hub, you can:

  • 👀 See live sessions: Watch what your teammates' AI is working on right now.
  • 💬 Message each other: Send quick notes or questions directly to teammates inside the app.
  • 🤖 Let AIs talk: Enable automatic conversation between your AI agents — they can share context and help each other solve problems.
  • 🔒 Backup everything: Every session is automatically stored and encrypted on your devices.

The best part? There's no server in the middle. It's peer-to-peer, meaning your data goes directly from your computer to your teammates' computers. And it's end-to-end encrypted, so only you and your teammates can see what's inside.

🔄 How Does It Work? (In Simple Terms)

When you and your teammates open Claude Code or Cursor, session-hub quietly connects to them. It collects small bits of information about what each AI session is doing (like a status update) and shares that securely with your group.

You'll see that information in a simple panel right inside your VS Code or Cursor editor. It's like a live activity feed for your team's AI work.

Since it's peer-to-peer, there's no central server storing anything. Your conversations and session data hop directly between devices using a smart networking technology called Hyperswarm.

✨ Key Features

🔐 End-to-End Encryption

All data shared between you and your teammates is fully encrypted on your device before transmission. Only intended recipients can decrypt and read it.

💾 Automatic Backup

Every session is automatically saved and timestamped on your own computer. That means you'll always have a copy, even if something goes wrong elsewhere.

🤝 P2P Collaboration

No central servers. Your computers talk directly to each other securely through the internet. Works seamlessly even across different networks.

🧠 MCP Server Built-In

Includes a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, which lets your AI agents communicate with session-hub and share structured context easily.

🛠️ Quick Troubleshooting Help

Here are a few common things that might trip you up, and how to fix them:

  • 🔘 I can't see the panel: Make sure you have VS Code or Cursor open and that session-hub is enabled in the extensions view (the puzzle piece icon).
  • 🔘 My teammates don't show up: Double-check that everyone is on the same version and that the team invite link was shared correctly.
  • 🔘 Nothing is backing up: Check your local folder permissions — session-hub needs permission to read/write in that location.
  • 🔘 Firewall warnings: When Windows asks, allow session-hub to communicate on private and public networks — needed for P2P to work.

If you get stuck, re-download the latest version from the link above — often, the newest release fixes issues.
